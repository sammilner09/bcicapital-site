How our bots & playbooks work

From messy workflow to a repeatable runbook.

We don’t drop a generic “AI assistant” into your inbox. We turn a real weekly pain into a named playbook your team can run.

Playbook

Map the grind

Sit with the people doing the work. List every repetitive step — invoices, filing, follow-ups, status checks — and where time and errors pile up.

Bot

Automate the top jobs

Build focused bots for the two or three highest-payoff tasks. Each one has a clear trigger, rules, and a human approval gate when money or customers are involved.

Hand-off

Train & improve

Your crew gets documentation and training. We watch the first weeks together, then leave you with tools you own — and a path to add the next bot.

Inside BCI companies

When BCI acquires a business, automation is part of how we strengthen it from day one. It trims the paperwork, tightens the books, and gives owner-employees better information to run on — which is how we help future-proof the shop itself.

  • Cleaner financials that are easier to verify
  • Staff trained to run and improve the tools themselves
  • People stay in their jobs. Automation handles the paperwork, not the people.

Our model

How a BCI deal works, in three steps.

We buy real, operating businesses with proven earnings from owners ready to retire. Then we put the business on a path to the people who run it — one dedicated ownership track, not the whole site’s story.

1

Find

We look for established, profitable local businesses whose owners are ready to retire and want the company to stay local.

2

Buy & strengthen

We verify earnings and customers, buy, then operate and improve the business with better systems, automation, and employee preparation.

3

Employees become owners

Employees get a path to own the business, through an employee stock ownership plan (ESOP), so the people who built it end up owning it.

What does employee-owned mean?

The people who do the work own a real stake in the company. That ownership sits on top of a fair paycheck, not in place of one.
