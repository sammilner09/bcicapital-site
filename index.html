How it works

Four steps

  1. Step 1

    Find strong businesses

    We look for high-profit, risk-managed businesses with steady customers and good teams.

  2. Step 2

    Learn from the team

    We get to know how the business runs day to day, working alongside the people who run it.

  3. Step 3

    Automate the back office

    Raven Automation handles routine back-office work like paperwork and scheduling, to improve margins and profit.

    Automation supports our people. We don't automate jobs away.

  4. Step 4

    Grow together

    We keep improving the business, with a planned path toward employee ownership.
