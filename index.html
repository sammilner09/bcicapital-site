Against the institution

The boardroom wasn't built for work boots. We're bringing them in anyway.

For a generation, the playbook was fixed: a retiring owner sells, a fund or a chain buys, the sign changes, the jobs thin out, and the profit leaves town. The people who actually built the business get a farewell cake.

BCI runs the opposite play. Employees at the ownership table — not just on the schedule. And the business ends up with the crew inside the building, not the highest bidder outside it.

Four ideas behind the look

Cutting-edge on the surface. Old values underneath.

Four ideas shape how this page looks — and how BCI thinks. Two are visual motifs: the chain link, for transparency and open documentation, and circuit line-work, for data-driven decisions. Two are the real model: employee ownership, and a philosophy that walks away from a broken system toward a better one.

MOTIF / 01

Open by design

The chain-link motif stands for transparency: documentation out in the open, financial information documented and verifiable, and nothing important hidden behind a curtain. If it's part of the deal, it should be on the record.

MOTIF / 02

Real businesses, not hype

The circuit motif stands for data-driven decisions: BCI looks at verified earnings and real customers first. No deal survives on a good story alone.

THE MODEL / 03

Employee owned

Our employees own the means of their production. Their measurement of production is the business itself — “the company” is the employees, and the path leads to employees owning the business through an ESOP.

THE MODEL / 04

Philosophically driven

“To exit” means to leave — in this case, to leave a broken economic system by joining a superior one. In that spirit, BCI builds ownership for the people who build the company, not only its owners.

How it works
Break the lock

How a BCI deal works — in three steps.

Private equity has operated behind closed doors for a long time. BCI does it differently, and in full view. BCI buys real, operating businesses — high-profit, risk-managed companies with proven earnings, not a vague fund or a speculative asset. BCI never goes public. Here is how it works:

STEP 01

We find established local businesses

BCI looks for profitable, established local businesses whose owners are ready to retire and want to see the company stay local.

STEP 02

We buy proven companies — and strengthen them

BCI studies the market, verifies the business — earnings, customer base — and buys only businesses with real earnings. We then operate and improve each one, while a nonprofit education arm prepares employees for ownership.

Disciplined buying. Hands-on operations. Employee preparation.
STEP 03

Employees buy in.

Employees get a path to owning the business through an ESOP (Employee Stock Ownership Plan), so the people who built it end up owning it.

The business stays with the crew inside the building

Who this is for

People who work hard to build local businesses — and want those businesses to stay local, with the crew who runs them getting a real path to owning them. BCI was designed for them.

Who we are

An employee-ownership-focused acquirer. We buy businesses to preserve them and transition them to their teams — not to close them, consolidate them, or sell them to the highest bidder.

What makes it different

The business goes to the employees, not to a chain or a larger fund. Ownership stays local and jobs stay local.

About the company

About BCI

BCI exists to give ownership to the people who build a company, not only to its owners. We acquire and grow high-profit, risk-managed businesses so that their employees become shareholders in the companies they help build.

We believe that when employees own a part of what they help create, America moves forward, united by shared purpose and shared ownership.
