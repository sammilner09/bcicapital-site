Meet Raven

AI will do the back office. Raven makes it work for your people.

Invoices, filing, follow-ups, reports: AI will do this work in every office. Raven is the AI BCI puts to work for its people, one job at a time, with a person signing off where money or customers are involved.

Back-office help, built one job at a time.

Raven is being built as practical help for routine admin work. It is not a generic assistant dropped into an inbox. Each tool is meant to do one clear job, with a person signing off where it matters.

Raven is being built for high-profit, risk-managed businesses. That includes outside businesses that want back-office help, and, as a plan, the businesses BCI plans to buy.

Automation supports our people. We don't automate jobs away.

Where Raven is headed

Coming soon

Raven learns how your company actually runs, from the owner's desk down to the loading dock, so when he's gone, nothing breaks.

Every company runs on one person's head. Tariffs, supplier calls, which new hire to trust with the big client. It's all in there, and none of it's written down. Raven sits in the workflow, earns that knowledge one approved task at a time, and becomes the system that knows what he knows. So the day he retires, or the day AI reshapes your workforce, the company doesn't flinch.

Raven is more than back-office automation. Over time it captures the owner's unwritten knowledge: how each job is done, the key relationships with employees, suppliers, and partners, and how outside risks like tariffs and competition hit the business. The company keeps running without the owner. Raven protects his legacy. It does not replace him.

It starts with one small job, one approved task at a time, and widens what it learns only with permission. The endgame is that Raven becomes the company's operating system. On the path to employee ownership, when the owner retires, the same system the team already uses is how they run the business themselves.

This is where Raven is headed. Coming soon.

Raven records only with permission. Your company owns its data.
