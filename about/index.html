Why we do it

Our north star: open-sourcing the entire US economy. When the people who do the work own the companies they build, the country moves forward together.

We plan for BCI to stay private, and never go public, so this mission stays with the people who started it.

BCI exists to give ownership to the people who build a company, alongside its owners and investors. We buy companies for the people: we acquire and grow businesses so the people who do the work have a path to become shareholders. When the people who do the work own part of what they create, America moves forward, united by shared purpose and shared ownership.

Well-known employee-owned companies include Publix, Hy-Vee, and SCHEELS. They are not affiliated with BCI.

72% of respondents would prefer to work for an employee-owned company (General Social Survey 2018, analyzed by Rutgers University and funded by the Employee Ownership Foundation). This is a general public finding, not a result from BCI.
