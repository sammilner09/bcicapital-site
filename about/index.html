About

We keep the shop in town, and we future-proof how it runs.

BCI buys proven local businesses from owners who are ready to retire. We strengthen the work with practical automation, and we open a path for the employees who run the business to hold a stake in it. That is how we future-proof the economy: the shop stays useful, and the value stays with the people who built it.

Three principals lead BCI. Control stays with them, so no outsider can steer a company away from that mission. Meet the team.
