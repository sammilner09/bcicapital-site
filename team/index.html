Who we are

Three founders. One answer to the AI era: ownership for the people who do the work.

Three principals. Control stays with us by design, so no outsider can steer a BCI company away from its mission.

CA

Casey Adams

Co-Founder & CEO

Casey is a decorated U.S. Army veteran and entrepreneur who ran TechMC and founded The Kansas City Drone Company. He has spent 15+ years turning complex technology into tools that work, from leading drone programs to building Ukraine's strategy for decentralized drone manufacturing. At BCI, he's focused on finding good businesses, running them well, and building a real path for the people who do the work to own a stake in what they build.

JA

Joe Allen IV

Co-Founder & Chief Marketing Officer

Joe leads marketing and outreach for BCI, building relationships with the business owners, employees, and communities it serves. He has lived in several countries around the world and has seen firsthand what happens when people stop pulling together. That experience is why he believes employee ownership can help unite America. A natural communicator and salesman, he's focused on making sure the people who do the work have a real path to owning it.

SM

Samuel Milner

Co-Founder & Chief Technology Officer

Samuel is a co-founder of BCI, based in Kansas, and he leads its AI work, from finding deals to running the shops we buy. He builds the automation, and he knows where it is heading: AI will do the computer work. That's why he's building BCI so the people who do the work get a real path to owning it.
