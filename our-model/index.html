Our Model

Employee ownership, in plain words.

An employee stock ownership plan (ESOP) is how a company can share ownership with the people who work there. BCI buys proven local businesses from owners who are ready to retire, strengthens them, and opens a path for the crew to hold a stake through a plan like this.

For a long time, buying a profitable private company sat with funds and people who were already wealthy. BCI does this work in the open, and stays private. The aim is a stake for the people who build the company, on top of the paycheck they already earn.

What does employee-owned mean?

The people who do the work hold a stake in the company through the plan. That stake sits on top of a fair paycheck. The paycheck still comes. The stake is extra, and it stays with the people who build the business.

Why it matters

The boardroom wasn't built for work boots. We're bringing them in anyway.

For a generation, the playbook stayed fixed. A retiring owner sells. A fund or a chain buys. The sign changes. Jobs thin out. Profit leaves town. The people who built the business get a farewell cake.

BCI runs the opposite play. Employees take a seat at the ownership table, as well as a line on the schedule. The business stays with the crew inside the building. Ownership stays in town.

BCI exists so the people who build a company can hold a stake in it, alongside the people who started it. When employees own a part of what they help create, a town keeps more of the value they built.

About 7 in 10 workers prefer employee-owned companies (71.5%, General Social Survey 2018), with support across parties (73% Democratic-leaning, 73% Republican-leaning, 65% independents).

How the stake is held

The plan holds the shares. People keep their paycheck.

In an employee stock ownership plan, company stock sits in a trust for employees. People take part through the plan. The stake is a benefit on top of wages.

Those shares stay in the plan. Each person does not receive stock they can sell on an open market. Who is included, and when someone is paid the value of their stake, is written into that company's plan when the company adopts it.

This page describes the path BCI is building. It promises no payout, no tax result, and no stake for every person on day one.
