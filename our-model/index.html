Employee ownership, in plain words.

Employee ownership is how a company shares ownership with the people who work there. BCI buys proven, high-profit, risk-managed local businesses from owners ready to step back, strengthens them, and opens the path for the crew to hold a stake. We buy companies for the people.

For generations, owning a profitable private company sat with people who were already wealthy. BCI does this work in the open, and stays private. A stake for the people who build the company, on top of the paycheck they already earn.

What does employee-owned mean?

The people who do the work hold a stake in the company through the plan. That stake sits on top of a fair paycheck. The paycheck still comes. The stake is extra, and it stays with the people who build the business.

How the stake is held

The plan holds the shares. People keep their paycheck.

In an employee ownership plan, company stock sits in a trust for employees. People take part through the plan. The stake is a benefit on top of wages.

Those shares stay in the plan. Each person does not receive stock they can sell on an open market. Who is included, and when someone is paid the value of their stake, is written into that company's plan when the company adopts it.
