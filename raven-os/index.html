Raven Automation

An AI-first operating system for the company.

Raven is the operating system and the company dashboard. Raven OS is a full operating system you install on company computers. People across the business use it under a company login. Executives sit down at a Raven desktop — laptop first, phone second — and see the company by department.

Raven works with leading AI models. BCI helps set the company up and match models to each workflow. Field staff get a bot for their role. The executive desktop is the place that brings the company together.

If your job can be done on a computer, AI is coming for it. That is BCI’s conviction. Employee ownership puts the people who do the work on the owning side when it happens. Raven is the AI BCI puts to work for its people.

Automation supports our people. We don’t automate jobs away.

The company desktop

Executives see the business at a high level, department by department, on a Raven OS desktop. The phone is a companion, not the main workplace.

A bot for each role

Each person connects email and their job software, and gets a bot for their role. Onboarding is meant to move down the organization from the people who lead each department.

Built for employee ownership

BCI buys high-profit, risk-managed businesses and builds the path for the people who do the work to own a stake. Raven is the operating system we put behind that work.

Getting started

Install and setup

Raven OS is a full operating system you install on company computers from a USB installer. This is a draft of the path, not a shipping product.

Step 1

Download the installer

Get the Raven OS installer from this page. In this draft, the button does not provide a file.

Step 2

Put it on a USB drive

Copy the installer to a USB drive so it can be used on company computers.

Step 3

Boot from the USB drive

Boot company computers from the USB drive to install Raven OS.

Step 4

First start

On first start, the owner sets up the company.

Step 5

Invite the staff

Invite links cascade to staff, who sign in with a company email.

Step 6

Connect the work

Each person connects email and their job software and gets a bot for their role.

Draft concept. Raven OS is shown here as a full operating system you install on company computers, not as an app that runs on another system. How this is described in public, including any comparison to other operating systems, needs attorney and IT review. This draft does not claim that Raven OS is available, that it runs on particular hardware, or that the software a company already uses will run on it.

Self-serve, plus a BCI setup call.
